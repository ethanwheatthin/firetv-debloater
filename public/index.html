Connect to your Fire TV

Your computer and Fire TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

How do I set up my Fire TV?
  1. Open Settings on your Fire TV and go to My Fire TV (sometimes under Device & Software).
  2. Choose About and click your device name (or Fire TV Stick) 7 times until it says you are a developer.
  3. Go back, open Developer Options, and turn ADB debugging on. Turn on Apps from Unknown Sources too if you see it.
  4. Find your IP address under About → Network and type it above.
  5. After you click Connect, look at the TV: choose Allow (tick "Always allow from this computer").

Menu names differ a little between Fire OS versions. Look for the closest match.

Choose apps to remove

The safest picks are already selected. Everything here can be restored later.

Careful. This lists every app on your Fire TV, not just known bloatware. Apps the Fire TV needs to start up, sign in, or show the home screen are locked and can't be changed, but other system apps can still break features (voice remote, updates, certain apps). Change a few at a time, restart the TV between changes, and prefer Disable.
